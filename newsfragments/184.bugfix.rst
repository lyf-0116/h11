The backing storage of :class:`Headers` is now an immutable tuple, matching
the frozen nature of event objects. Body framing (Content-Length, chunked, or
HTTP/1.0 end-of-file) is derived once when the ``Request``/``Response`` is
processed; freezing the headers prevents code from mutating them afterwards
and desynchronizing the framing actually put on the wire from the framing
the peer was told to expect.
